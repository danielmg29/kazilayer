# KaziLayer visual direction

## Design fingerprint

KaziLayer is calm, decisive, structured, and quietly technical. Its visual blend is 70 percent Swiss technical minimalism, 20 percent modular bento composition, and 10 percent blueprint and flow graphics.

- Composition: strict twelve-column logic, strong alignment, dense operational content, and asymmetric emphasis instead of a uniform card grid.
- Typography: Manrope for product headings and Inter for interface text. Headings are compact and confident; labels are concise and often use tracked uppercase.
- Colour: Kazi indigo is the authority colour, mint marks flow and successful system state, ink carries readable hierarchy, and cool cloud surfaces keep dense screens calm.
- Shape: mostly square geometry with 8px and 14px radii; 24px is reserved for large brand surfaces. Pills are limited to status and compact controls.
- Surfaces: flat or one-pixel bordered by default. Elevation is sparse and used for overlays, not every panel.
- Spacing: a 4px base rhythm with compact operational density and deliberate larger gaps between page regions.
- Graphics: thin blueprint lines, nodes, route paths, grid coordinates, and measurement marks. They explain system flow rather than decorate empty space.
- Motion: short, mechanical transitions with low travel; all nonessential movement stops under reduced-motion preferences.

## Avoid

Avoid generic glowing SaaS gradients, glass panels, oversized marketing copy, decorative icon circles, excessive pills, uniform rounded cards, and invented metrics unrelated to the product contract.

## Recipe axes

Shared components use `intent`, `density`, `tone`, and `surface`. Content enters through semantic slots (`title`, `subtitle`, `badge`, `media`, `meta`, `timestamp`, `owner`, `actions`). Narrow lists explicitly choose scroll, collapse, or priority behaviour.

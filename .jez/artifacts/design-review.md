# Design Review: KaziLayer Landing Page
**Date**: 2026-10-09  
**URL**: http://localhost:3000/

## Overall impression

The first landing-page pass carried KaziLayer's palette but leaned too heavily on oversized SaaS display text, an equal-weight bento composition and a large mint colour field. The revised direction is more disciplined and product-specific: a white operational header, a measured two-line hero, annotated interface captures and motion that describes work moving through the system.

## Findings addressed

### High

- **Display type was compressed** — the hero combined an oversized font, narrow column and `.84` line-height. The measure, scale, tracking and leading are now coordinated at desktop and mobile sizes.
- **Logo looked like a pasted asset** — the wide lockup sat inside an arbitrary white rectangle. It is now reconstructed from the approved mark with a typographic name/tagline lockup in a full-width navigation rail.

### Medium

- **Motion was too subtle to register** — one 12px fade was reused everywhere. The page now uses distinct rise, lateral and focused-media reveals, plus a continuous request-to-verification signal rail.
- **Accent colour overwhelmed the visual ratio** — the full mint interstitial read as a template device. Mint is now limited to flow, state and emphasis signals.
- **Product presentation felt like a generic bento grid** — stronger annotations, a quieter frame treatment and staged media reveals make the interface captures read as operational evidence rather than decorative cards.

## Patterns preserved

- Kazi Indigo remains the authority surface.
- Flow Mint continues to identify movement and live state.
- Square geometry, technical labels and one-pixel rules retain the Swiss/blueprint fingerprint.
- Motion stays transform/opacity/clip based and respects reduced-motion preferences.
